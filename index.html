    🚨 Emergency Cyber Crime Help & Helpline

    If you have lost money or entered passwords, act immediately within the first 1-2 hours ('Golden Hour').

    National Cyber Crime Helpline (India)
    1930

    Toll-free emergency helpline available 24x7 to block fraudulent transactions immediately.

    📞 Call 1930 Helpline

    Immediate 4-Step Emergency Action Plan:

    1. Call 1930 Helpline

    Report the transaction immediately so cyberpolice can freeze the scammer's bank account.

    2. Contact Your Bank

    Call your bank's customer care to block your ATM card, net banking, and UPI ID.

    3. File Official Complaint

    Submit transaction reference number and screenshots on cybercrime.gov.in or local police station.

    4. Change All Passwords

    Change your UPI PIN, banking passwords, and email credentials immediately.
